    How the score works

    Florida rows were read from public Zillow pages, plus some earlier Redfin pages.

    Texas rows were read from public Zillow pages. A few earlier Texas listings were read from Redfin.

    Colorado rows were read from public Zillow price-reduced lists. Some were checked again on the Redfin listing page.

    Each card shows only categories the source actually printed. Nothing is guessed. The total stops at 100.

    1. Latest price cut: percent times 2.3, up to 25. A 10% cut is 23. About 10.9% or more is 25. No cut is 0.
    2. Number of cuts on this listing: 5 each, up to 15.
    3. Days on market: days divided by 12, up to 10. About 120 days or more is 10. Uses the printed days-on-market figure when the page has one. Otherwise uses days on Zillow or days on Redfin, whichever that page printed. Uses the printed days-on-market figure when the page has one. Otherwise uses days on Zillow, because these rows were read from Zillow. The earlier Redfin listings use days on Redfin. Uses the printed days-on-market figure when the page has one. Otherwise uses days on Zillow. Listings checked on a Redfin page use the day count printed there.
    4. Drop from the first ask on this listing: 1 point per percent, up to 10.
    5. Off market, then back, or expired and relisted: 12.
    6. Relisted under the prior ask: 8 more.
    7. Pending or contingent, then back on the market: 12. More than once is mentioned, not extra points.
    8. Under the tax assessment or under the last sale: 6.
    9. Vacant, moved, tenant, estate, divorce, relocation, or seller-financing language: 4.
    10. Remarks say motivated, bring offers, price improved, or similar: 4.
    11. Agent replaced, or a refresh with no real change: 4.
    12. Nearby homes went under contract, or a cut right after a dead open house: 4.
