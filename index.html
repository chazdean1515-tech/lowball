How the score works

Each card shows only categories the source actually printed. Nothing is guessed. The total stops at 100.

  1. Latest price cut: percent times 2.3, up to 25. A 10% cut is 23. About 10.9% or more is 25. No cut is 0.
  2. Number of cuts on this listing: 5 each, up to 15.
  3. Days on market: days divided by 12, up to 10. About 120 days or more is 10. Uses the printed days-on-market figure, otherwise days on Redfin or Zillow.
  4. Drop from the first ask on this listing: 1 point per percent, up to 10.
  5. Off market, then back, or expired and relisted: 12.
  6. Relisted under the prior ask: 8 more.
  7. Pending or contingent, then back on the market: 12. More than once is noted, not extra points.
  8. Under the tax assessment or under the last sale: 6.
  9. Vacant, moved, tenant, estate, divorce, relocation, or seller-financing language: 4. None of these pages earned it.
  10. Remarks say motivated, bring offers, price improved, or similar: 4.
  11. Agent replaced, or a refresh with no real change: 4. Not shown here.
  12. Nearby homes went under contract, or a cut right after a dead open house: 4. Not shown here.
