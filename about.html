About

Lowball ranks motivated home sellers in Florida, Texas, and Colorado. The score uses price cuts, days on market, relists, and failed contracts (a pending or contingent sale that came back on the market) when the public listing page printed that signal. Nothing on a card is guessed. The total stops at 100.

The score is Lowball's own ranking. It is not an appraisal, and it is not a field from a brokerage or an MLS.

Where the rows come from

Florida and Texas rows were read from public Zillow pages, plus some earlier Redfin pages. Colorado rows were read from public Zillow price-reduced lists, and some of those were checked again on the Redfin listing page. Days on market use the figure that page printed: days on market when it is shown, otherwise days on Zillow, or days on Redfin for the earlier Redfin rows.

Each board shows the day that batch was fetched. A listing shows its own fetched day when one was recorded. The boards are not a live feed.

Lowball is not affiliated with Redfin, Zillow, or any MLS.
