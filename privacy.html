Privacy

Effective October 6, 2026.

Lowball is a static website. Search and the state tabs run in your browser. The pages do not ask you to create an account, and they do not include analytics or advertising scripts.

These pages do not set cookies. The newsletter signup does not load an outside form until it is turned on. After that, the email provider’s form may set its own cookies. Lowball still does not run analytics.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs, such as an IP address, the browser type, and the page requested. Lowball does not operate that host and does not control those logs. GitHub describes what it collects in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When the city map is on screen, the browser requests map pictures from OpenStreetMap at tile.openstreetmap.org. That request sends your IP address and this page's address to OpenStreetMap's tile servers. The map waits until you scroll it into view or open it from the Map link, so those requests do not start just because the page loaded. OpenStreetMap describes tile use in its tile usage policy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Links on a listing go to Redfin or Zillow. The moving quote goes to ScanToMove, an affiliate partner. Those sites have their own privacy practices. ScanToMove sets its own cookies once you click that link. These Lowball pages do not set cookies.

Free weekly newsletter

The board has a signup labeled “Subscribe to the free weekly newsletter.” It asks for an email address. That address is collected only to send the free weekly newsletter. Lowball does not sell it. The list is not open yet, so the form does not save or send the address until a newsletter provider is connected.

When a message is sent, it will say it is from Lowball, include a postal address, and include a link that unsubscribes that address in one step. Lowball will honor an unsubscribe within 10 business days. You can also email contact@uselowball.com with “Unsubscribe” in the subject and ask to be removed.

To reach Lowball, email contact@uselowball.com. Do not send account numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information in that email.

Lowball is not affiliated with Redfin, Zillow, or any MLS. The boards are not a live feed.
